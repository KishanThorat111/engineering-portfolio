---
title: 'The plan limits the reporting, never the diners'
system: 'menu-platform'
decision: >-
  When a tenant reaches the daily unique-visitor allowance of its plan, the menu carries on
  loading for everyone. What stops is the counter: further visits simply are not recorded
  against that day's analytics.
why: >-
  The person scanning the code is a diner at a table, not the customer who bought the
  subscription. Serving them an upgrade prompt, or nothing at all, would punish someone with
  no relationship to the plan and embarrass the restaurant in front of them. The plan should
  meter the reporting the restaurant is paying for, not the meal.
tradeoff: >-
  It gives up the most direct upgrade pressure a tiered plan has, and it means the analytics a
  capped tenant sees are deliberately incomplete — so the number has to be presented as a
  capped figure rather than a total, or it becomes quietly misleading.
source:

  ref: '§9.1 plan matrix and §9.3 soft cap on analytics, not traffic'
